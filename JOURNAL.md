# Journal

KEEB verifies hours from this file. Each entry has:

- **date**
- **hours** (required — an entry without hours does not count)
- **what I did**
- **what I got stuck on**
- **photos** in `journal/photos/` (every 1–4 hours when there is something to shoot)

Optional tracker: [Lapse](https://lapse.hackclub.com/).
